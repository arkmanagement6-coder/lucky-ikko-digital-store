Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains the operational timelines and delivery frameworks for the services offered by ARK Digital (managed by ARK Management, "we," "our," "us").

Because we operate strictly as a digital marketing and search visibility agency, all deliverables and services purchased through our platform or payment networks are **classified as digital services**.

1. Shipping Charges (Zero Fees)

Since our services are delivered online, there are **no physical shipping fees**, logistics charges, customs duties, or transport costs of any kind associated with your transactions.

2. Delivery Methods

Our digital marketing services are delivered through electronic and virtual mediums:

  • Digital Reports & Audits: Transmitted directly to your registered email address in PDF or cloud formats (Google Sheets, Looker Studio).
  • Campaign Setups: Executed directly on your business assets (Google Business Profile, Google Ads Manager, Meta Business Manager, Search Console) after authorized access is shared.
  • PR & Media Placements: Published as digital articles on live, indexable third-party media news portals.
  • Consultations: Conducted via remote video conferencing tools (Zoom, Google Meet) or telephone.

3. Delivery Timelines

Delivery timelines vary based on the specific service package purchased, and are outlined as follows:

  • Consultations & Strategy Audits: Arranged and scheduled within 24 to 48 hours of receipt of payment and brief details.
  • Google Listing Setup: Optimization and verification steps begin within 2 to 3 business days. Final Google indexing depends on local review and verification protocols.
  • Paid Advertising Campaigns: Creative copy, audience segments, and funnel assets are prepared for launch within 5 to 7 business days.
  • SEO Campaigns (Recurring): Initial keyword mappings and technical fixes are delivered within 14 business days. On-going optimization and link building are delivered steadily throughout each billing cycle month.
  • PR Placement: Copy creation takes 3 to 5 business days. Publications go live within 7 to 10 business days after approval.

4. Delivery Confirmation

A service cycle is deemed "delivered" once the monthly campaign performance report, link acquisition details, active ad campaign metrics, or live PR links are sent to your registered email.
