Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

At ARK Digital (managed by ARK Management, "we," "our," "us"), we provide professional digital services including Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Paid Ads Management, Google Listing setup, and PR placement campaigns. Because these services involve direct intellectual labor, creative copywriting, search configurations, and external media ad spend, we maintain a clear Refund & Cancellation Policy.

1. Service Cancellations

Clients are free to cancel or pause their recurring monthly marketing campaigns at any point. To avoid auto-billing for the subsequent cycle:

  • You must submit a written cancellation request via email to arkmanagement6@gmail.com at least 10 business days before your next scheduled invoice/billing date.
  • Once cancellation is processed, we will complete the current month's campaign work, and no further charges will be made.

2. Refund Terms

Please review our refund parameters carefully before committing to a campaign:

  • Work in Progress: Once campaign setup, keyword mapping, or ad copy creation has initiated, fees paid for that monthly cycle are non-refundable.
  • Third-Party Ad Spend: Any money transferred directly into advertising accounts (Google Ads, Meta Business Manager) is spent on third-party ad networks. We cannot issue refunds for ad spend consumed by search networks or social networks.
  • PR & Media Placements: Placements on news portals and PR platforms are permanent editorial writeups and cannot be recalled or refunded once published.
  • Service Dissatisfaction: If you are dissatisfied with campaign results, please contact your account manager immediately. We will review our data audits and adjust search parameters, copy, or bids, but past service cycles will not be refunded.

3. Pro-Rated Refunds

If a project is terminated mid-cycle by ARK Digital due to structural issues, resource adjustments, or conflicts of interest (and not due to client contract violations), we will calculate a pro-rated refund for the remaining days of that billing month and process it back to the original payment source.

4. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed through our payment gateway provider (such as Razorpay). Refunds will be credited back to your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI) within 5 to 7 business days of approval, depending on the banking network's processing times.
