Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our agency model and onboarding process.

What is your campaign onboarding duration?

For SEO and Google Listings, optimization starts within 48 hours of asset access collection. For Paid Ads, initial analytics setup and copy creation take roughly 5-7 business days before launch.

Do you charge setup fees for advertising?

We operate on a transparent fixed monthly management retainer. There are no surprise setup fees or hidden charges unless customized landing page development is explicitly contracted.

How do we communicate during a campaign?

We coordinate everything digitally. Communication is handled via email support and scheduled virtual video consultations, ensuring smooth campaign tracking from any location.
