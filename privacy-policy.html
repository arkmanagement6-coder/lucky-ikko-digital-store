Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

At ARK Digital (managed by ARK Management, "we," "our," "us"), we prioritize the privacy and security of our clients, visitors, and partners. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, safeguard, and use your personal information when you access index.html or engage us for digital marketing services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit queries, fill out forms, or interact with our services:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, business name, and job title.
  • Contact Data: Email address, mobile/telephone number, and registered physical address.
  • Service Preference Data: Your website URL, existing marketing channels, and selected service packages.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser settings, and page engagement patterns collected via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your information to serve your marketing campaigns and maintain website security, specifically:

  • To compile and deliver custom marketing audits and service quotes.
  • To manage our client relationship, compile invoices, and process monthly service renewals.
  • To verify transaction records processed via secure payment networks (e.g., Razorpay).
  • To analyze site traffic, optimize layout speed, and secure our network from malicious bots.

3. Payment Gateway & Third-Party Processors

We do not store your credit card, debit card, or banking credentials on our local servers. All payment flows are processed securely through certified payment gateways (such as Razorpay).

These gateways comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS) to ensure that your financial details are processed with absolute confidentiality.

4. Data Retention & Safeguards

We store your business identities and marketing briefs in secure, encrypted cloud environments. Access to client properties is restricted only to active strategists assigned to your campaign.

We retain client campaign records only as long as needed to fulfill tax obligations and active marketing service arrangements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, edit, or ask for the deletion of your personal records from our database. To make a query or delete your identity logs, please write to us at the email listed below.

6. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon their posting to this page.
